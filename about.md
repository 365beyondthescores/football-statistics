# About 365BeyondTheScores

## Who We Are

365BeyondTheScores is an independent digital media and knowledge project focused on sports, football statistics, analytics, and educational resources.

The project explores the information behind sports results and explains statistical concepts in a structured and accessible way.

> **Beyond the Score. Discover the Story.**

## Our Focus

365BeyondTheScores focuses on:

- Football statistics
- Football analytics
- Match performance
- Player performance
- Sports data
- Statistical concepts
- Educational resources

## Football Statistics Knowledge Hub

The Football Statistics Knowledge Hub is a structured collection of educational resources explaining common football statistics and analytical concepts.

The collection currently covers topics including:

- Expected Goals (xG)
- Expected Assists (xA)
- Ball Possession
- Shots
- Shots on Target
- Passing Accuracy
- Clean Sheets
- Key Passes
- Progressive Passes
- Player Ratings

Explore the [Football Statistics Knowledge Hub](README.md).

## Editorial Approach

365BeyondTheScores aims to explain statistical concepts clearly while acknowledging that different football-data providers may use different definitions, datasets, event classifications, and methodologies.

We avoid presenting one provider's proprietary methodology as a universal definition.

Where appropriate, external references and further reading are provided through the [References & Methodology](references.md) page.

## About This Repository

This repository organizes football-statistics concepts into a structured knowledge resource.

The content is intended for informational and educational purposes.

Statistics should always be interpreted according to their source, definition, methodology, and match context.
