# What Is Passing Accuracy in Football?

## Definition

Passing accuracy is a football statistic that represents the percentage of attempted passes that are successfully completed.

It is commonly used to evaluate a team's or player's passing efficiency.

## How Is Passing Accuracy Calculated?

A simple calculation is:

**Passing Accuracy = Completed Passes ÷ Attempted Passes × 100**

For example, if a player attempts 100 passes and successfully completes 90 of them:

**90 ÷ 100 × 100 = 90% passing accuracy**

The exact definitions of attempted and completed passes can vary between statistical providers.

## Example

Consider two players:

- Player A: 90 completed passes from 100 attempts = 90%
- Player B: 45 completed passes from 50 attempts = 90%

Both players have the same passing accuracy.

However, Player A completed more passes overall.

This demonstrates why passing accuracy should be considered alongside passing volume and match context.

## Why Is Passing Accuracy Important?

Passing accuracy can help provide information about:

- Ball retention
- Passing efficiency
- Distribution
- Build-up play
- Technical performance
- Team possession patterns

However, a high passing accuracy does not automatically mean that a player or team performed better.

## Passing Accuracy and Passing Risk

Not every pass has the same level of difficulty.

Short passes in safe areas are generally easier to complete than long passes, forward passes under pressure, or passes attempting to break defensive lines.

A player who attempts more difficult progressive passes may have a lower passing accuracy while still making an important contribution to attacking play.

## Passing Accuracy and Possession

Passing accuracy can be related to possession because successful passes can help a team maintain control of the ball.

However, possession and passing accuracy measure different things.

**Possession** measures control of the ball.

**Passing accuracy** measures the proportion of completed passes among attempted passes.

## Passing Accuracy and Attacking Performance

Passing accuracy alone does not measure how effectively a team creates chances.

For example, a team can complete many safe passes without creating significant attacking opportunities.

Other statistics can provide additional context:

- Key passes
- Expected Assists (xA)
- Progressive passes
- Shots
- Expected Goals (xG)
- Final-third entries

## Example of Different Passing Profiles

Consider two players:

### Player A

- 95% passing accuracy
- Mostly short passes
- Few forward passes

### Player B

- 82% passing accuracy
- More forward passes
- More passes into dangerous areas

Player A has higher passing accuracy, but Player B may contribute more directly to progressing the ball or creating attacking opportunities.

This is why passing accuracy should be interpreted together with the player's role and tactical responsibilities.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is passing accuracy?

Passing accuracy is the percentage of attempted passes that are successfully completed.

### How is passing accuracy calculated?

It can be calculated by dividing completed passes by attempted passes and multiplying the result by 100.

### Is higher passing accuracy always better?

No.

A high passing accuracy can indicate efficient passing, but it does not measure the difficulty, purpose, or attacking value of those passes.

### Can a player have lower passing accuracy but still perform well?

Yes.

Players who frequently attempt progressive, creative, or difficult passes may have lower passing accuracy while still making valuable contributions.

### Does passing accuracy determine possession?

No.

Passing accuracy can contribute to ball retention, but possession is a separate statistic.

### Is passing accuracy calculated the same way by every provider?

Not necessarily.

Different data providers may use different definitions and data collection methods.

## How to Interpret Passing Accuracy

Passing accuracy becomes more useful when combined with:

**Passing Accuracy → Passing Volume → Pass Direction → Progression → Chance Creation**

This provides a broader view of passing performance than accuracy alone.

## Data Methodology

Football statistics can differ between data providers because they may use different event definitions, data sources, and collection methodologies.

When comparing statistics, consider the source and methodology.

## Summary

Passing accuracy is a useful statistic for understanding how frequently attempted passes are completed.

However, accuracy alone does not describe the complete value of a player's passing.

The best interpretation considers passing accuracy together with volume, difficulty, direction, progression, tactical role, and match context.
