# What Are Shots on Target in Football?

## Definition

Shots on target are attempts that are directed toward the goal and would result in a goal if they were not stopped by the goalkeeper or another relevant defensive intervention.

A shot that results in a goal is also generally counted as a shot on target.

The exact definition can vary between statistical providers.

## Shots vs Shots on Target

Shots represent the total number of recorded attempts.

Shots on target are a subset of those attempts that meet the relevant definition of being directed toward the goal.

For example:

- Team A: 15 shots
- Team A: 6 shots on target

This means 6 of the team's 15 recorded attempts were classified as shots on target.

## Examples of Shots on Target

Depending on the statistical provider, examples can include:

- A shot saved by the goalkeeper
- A shot that results in a goal
- A shot stopped by a defender directly on the goal line

Definitions may differ between data providers, so the methodology should always be considered.

## Examples That Are Usually Not Shots on Target

Attempts that go:

- Wide of the goal
- Over the goal
- Into the stands
- Directly into a defender without being directed toward the goal

are generally not classified as shots on target.

## Why Are Shots on Target Important?

Shots on target can provide additional information about attacking accuracy.

They can help analysts understand:

- Shooting accuracy
- Attacking pressure
- Goalkeeper workload
- Offensive efficiency
- Difference between shot volume and shot accuracy

However, shots on target alone do not measure the quality of scoring opportunities.

## Example

Consider two teams:

- Team A: 18 shots, 4 shots on target
- Team B: 9 shots, 6 shots on target

Team A attempted more shots.

Team B recorded more shots on target.

This shows that total shot volume and shooting accuracy can tell different stories.

## Shots on Target and xG

Shots on target can be useful when analyzed alongside Expected Goals (xG).

For example:

- Team A: 10 shots, 7 on target, 0.8 xG
- Team B: 7 shots, 4 on target, 1.6 xG

Team A had more accurate attempts, but Team B generated greater expected goal value according to the model.

This demonstrates why shot accuracy and shot quality are different concepts.

## Shots on Target and Possession

Possession provides context about ball control, while shots on target provide information about attempts directed toward the goal.

A team can have high possession but relatively few shots on target.

Another team may have less possession but create several dangerous opportunities through counter-attacks.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a shot on target?

A shot on target is generally an attempt directed toward the goal that would enter the goal if it were not stopped by the goalkeeper or another relevant defensive intervention.

### Does a goal count as a shot on target?

Yes, a goal is generally counted as a shot on target.

### Is a shot that hits the post a shot on target?

This can depend on the statistical provider's definition. A shot that hits the post may not be classified as a shot on target if it does not meet the provider's criteria.

### Does a blocked shot count as a shot on target?

Usually, a shot blocked by an outfield player before reaching the goal is not classified as a shot on target. Definitions can vary by provider.

### Does more shots on target mean a team will win?

No.

Shots on target are one performance indicator and do not guarantee a particular match result.

## How to Interpret Shots on Target

A broader analysis can combine:

**Shots → Shots on Target → Shot Quality → xG → Goals**

This provides more context than looking at shooting accuracy alone.

## Data Methodology

Football statistics can vary between providers because they may use different data sources, event definitions, and collection methods.

When comparing statistics, always consider the source and methodology.

## Summary

Shots on target are an important indicator of shooting accuracy and attacking activity.

However, they should be considered alongside total shots, shot quality, Expected Goals (xG), possession, tactics, and match context.
