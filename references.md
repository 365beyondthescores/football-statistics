# Methodology Disclaimer

Football statistics are not always standardized across providers.

Statistics such as xG, xA, shots, shots on target, passing accuracy, key passes, progressive passes, possession, and player ratings may be calculated or classified differently depending on the data source and methodology.

Therefore:

**Same match + different provider ≠ necessarily same statistic**

When comparing statistics, always identify:

1. The data provider
2. The definition of the statistic
3. The underlying data
4. The methodology
5. The relevant competition and match context

The explanations in this repository describe general football-statistics concepts and are not intended to reproduce proprietary models or methodologies belonging to any specific provider.
